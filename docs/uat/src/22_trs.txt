# 17. Treasury and cash management (SRS 6.14)
@release R1

## Industry context
Treasury decides **when and how** money leaves the company. In SERK the system **never moves money itself**: it prepares a payment instruction, two or three different people approve and release it, the instruction goes to the bank (as a file or by the bank's own portal), and only when the **bank statement** shows the debit does the system mark the payment as settled. This matters because "released" in the ERP does not mean "paid": a bank may reject a payment, and a fraudster could otherwise mark an invoice as paid without any money moving.

Cash is the lifeblood of a contractor. The client pays 45-60 days after the work, but wages and suppliers must be paid sooner, so treasury maintains a **13-week cash forecast** that separates confirmed flows (approved invoices, certified IPCs) from estimated ones (expected IPC receipts, payroll, tax).

@term Payment instruction = A record telling the bank to pay; it is not proof of payment.
@term Settlement = The bank confirming (on a statement) that the money left or arrived.
@term pain.001 / camt.053 / MT940 = Standard bank file formats for payment instructions and account statements.
@term Letter of credit = A bank's commitment to pay a foreign supplier on presentation of shipping documents.

@case T-TRS-01 | Payments are prepared, approved and released by three different people with fresh MFA | R1 | TRS-01, IAM-08, IAM-04 | TRS, CFO, AP
pre: Approved payable to SC-01 for 3,400,000 (T-SUB-02). Dual-approval threshold 1,000,000.
1. As trs.a prepare the payment run containing this payable.
2. As trs.a try to approve it. As fin.b (finance approver) approve without fresh MFA. Then with fresh MFA.
3. Because the amount exceeds the threshold, have a second approver (cfo.a) approve with fresh MFA.
4. As trs.a (the preparer) try to release; then release as trs.b (treasury manager) with fresh MFA.
5. Inspect what the system now says about the payment and the invoice.
=> Self-approval and self-release are refused. Approval without fresh MFA is refused. Above the threshold two distinct approvers are required, and neither is the preparer or the releaser.
=> After release the payment status is "Released / sent to bank", **not "Paid"**; the invoice remains open until settlement.
=> Preparer, approvers and releaser are recorded; each decision shows the attestation/MFA time.
why: In every large payment fraud, one person could move money alone.

@case T-TRS-02 | Settlement comes only from the imported bank statement, by an independent person | R1 | TRS-01, FIN-05 | TRS, FIN
pre: Statement file from the bank (CSV, or MT940/camt.053 if available) containing the debit of 3,400,000 and a bank fee of 500.
1. As trs.c (bank statement officer, who holds no payment permission) import the statement.
2. Check how the payment of 3,400,000 is matched.
3. Try to mark the payment "paid" manually.
4. Reconcile the bank account.
=> The import is recorded with file name, hash, user and time. The statement line is matched to the payment, which becomes **Settled** only now, and the invoice leaves AP.
=> Step 3 is impossible: there is no manual "mark as paid".
=> The 500 bank fee appears as an unmatched line to be coded; the bank reconciliation shows zero unexplained difference after coding.
=> An unmatched or rejected payment remains open, visible and cannot be silently closed.

@case T-TRS-03 | The 13-week cash forecast separates confirmed from forecast flows | R1 | TRS-02 | TRS, CFO, EXE
1. Open the 13-week forecast.
2. Check the weekly lines: payroll, taxes, supplier payments by due date, expected IPC receipts, debt service.
3. Approve an additional supplier payment run and refresh; certify an IPC and refresh.
=> Confirmed flows (approved payables, certified IPC receivables) are visually separated from estimated flows; the opening balance equals the bank balance.
=> The forecast moves when documents are approved; the project-horizon forecast uses the same data.

@case T-TRS-04 | Payment files and bank statement formats | R2 | TRS-03, INT-05 | TRS
1. Export the approved run as an ISO 20022 pain.001 file.
2. Import statements in camt.053, MT940 and bank CSV.
=> The pain.001 file validates against the standard and totals match the run; the import of each statement format yields identical matching results; files are attested on import (hash, user, time).
note: "Should": mark Blocked if the bank chosen for release 1 does not support files (decision D-08).

@case T-TRS-05 | Bank facilities, overdrafts and guarantee lines | R2 | TRS-04, CON-07 | TRS, CFO
1. Record overdraft facility 10,000,000 and a guarantee line 25,000,000; the advance guarantee (12,000,000) and performance bond (6,000,000) draw on the guarantee line.
=> Utilisation shown: guarantee line used 18,000,000 (72%), available 7,000,000; overdraft used from the bank balance. Alerts at the configured limit.

@case T-TRS-06 | Letters of credit for imports | R2 | TRS-05, PRC-06 | TRS, PROC
1. Open an LC for the import order of T-PRC-06 (amount USD 52,500): application, issue, amendment, shipping presentation, payment.
=> Each LC stage is recorded with documents; the LC links to the import order; settlement happens only through the bank statement.

@case T-TRS-07 | Site petty cash on an imprest basis | R3 | TRS-06 | SITE, FIN
pre: Imprest float 50,000 at site P-101.
1. Record expenses of 38,500 with receipts.
2. Request replenishment.
=> The replenishment request is exactly **38,500** (float restored to 50,000), approved by someone other than the custodian, and cash on hand plus receipts always equals 50,000.
