# 12. Inventory and materials (SRS 6.9)
@release R1

## Industry context
Building materials are bulky, valuable and easy to lose: cement gets wet, rebar is stolen, sand is over-issued. A contractor runs a **central warehouse** and a **store on each site**, and moves material between them. Every movement has a document: **receipt** (goods in, against a PO), **issue** (goods out, charged to a project cost code), **transfer** (between stores) and **adjustment** (after a count). Stock is kept in a **base unit** (for example kilograms) even though it is bought in bags and tonnes.

Stock must be **valued**. When the same item is bought at different prices, the system uses **weighted average cost (WAC)** or **FIFO** per tenant policy; the policy decides what a project is charged and the stock subledger must always equal the inventory account in the general ledger, with **zero unexplained difference** (R1).

@term Base unit = The unit in which stock is stored (for example kg); purchase and issue units convert to it.
@term Stock count = Physical counting of stock and comparing with the system; differences become adjustments.
@term Reservation = Quantity set aside for a planned activity so it is not issued elsewhere.
@term Heat number = The steel mill's batch identifier for rebar, tied to its test certificate.
@term Wastage = Material used beyond the theoretical quantity in the BOQ.

@case T-INV-01 | Receipts, issues to cost codes, and approved transfers between stores | R1 | INV-01 | STORE, SITE, PM
pre: ST-101 holds 1,000 bags of cement at ETB 900 (fixture). W-01 central warehouse has 3,000 bags.
1. As store.a record a goods receipt of 400 bags at ETB 960 against a PO into ST-101.
2. Issue 300 bags to project P-101, cost code 03-100.
3. As store.b create a transfer of 500 bags from W-01 to ST-101; have it approved by a second person; dispatch it.
4. As store.a receive the transfer.
5. Try to change a posted receipt quantity.
=> Receipt increases ST-101 by 400 bags; issue reduces it by 300 and charges 03-100.
=> The transfer needs approval by someone other than the requester; during transport the stock shows "in transit" (not in either store); after receipt W-01 is 2,500 and ST-101 increases by 500.
=> The posted receipt is immutable (correction by adjustment/return only).

@case T-INV-02 | Stock is valued by the tenant policy and reconciles to the ledger with zero difference | R1 | INV-02, FIN-05, NFR-DAT-03 | STORE, FIN
pre: Policy WAC. Start: 1,000 bags at ETB 900. Record S0 (quantity) and V0 (value).
1. Receive 400 bags at ETB 960.
2. Issue 300 bags to 03-100.
3. Open the stock valuation and the reconciliation of stock subledger to the inventory control account.
4. Switch a second test tenant to FIFO, repeat steps 1-2.
=> After step 1: quantity 1,400 bags; value **1,284,000.00**; average cost **917.14** (917.142857 held at full precision).
=> Issue of 300 bags is charged at **275,142.86** to 03-100; remaining value **1,008,857.14** for 1,100 bags.
=> The subledger equals the inventory control account; the unexplained difference is exactly 0.00.
=> FIFO tenant: the 300 bags issued cost **270,000.00** (oldest layer at 900); remaining value **1,014,000.00** (700 bags at 900 plus 400 bags at 960).
why: A wrong stock value means a wrong cost on every job and a wrong balance sheet.

@case T-INV-03 | A stock count is approved by someone other than the counter, and the adjustment journal is posted by a third person | R1 | INV-03, IAM-08 | STORE, FIN
pre: System stock at ST-101 after T-INV-02: 1,400 bags (before the issue; or use the figure on your sheet), average cost 917.14.
1. As store.a start a count and enter 1,390 bags counted.
2. Submit; try to approve as store.a; approve as store.b.
3. Have fin.a post the generated adjustment journal; try the same as store.b.
=> Variance = -10 bags x 917.142857 = **-9,171.43** (loss), shown by item with the reason code.
=> Counter cannot approve; the second person can. The journal (debit stock loss / credit inventory) is balanced and must be posted by someone who neither counted nor approved.
=> After posting the stock quantity is 1,390 and the subledger still equals the control account.
why: Storekeepers who count their own stock and adjust it themselves can hide theft.

@case T-INV-04 | Units of measure convert correctly; stock is stored in the base unit | R1 | INV-04, ORG-07 | STORE, SITE
1. Receive 800 bags of cement. Open the stock card.
2. Issue 2.5 tonnes. Issue 30 kg.
3. Try to issue 1 bag of a product that has no conversion defined.
=> Stock card shows 40,000 kg (= 40 tonnes = 800 bags). Issue of 2.5 tonnes = 2,500 kg = 50 bags; 30 kg is accepted as 0.6 bag.
=> Step 3 is refused naming the missing conversion.

@case T-INV-05 | Material requests and reservations against schedule activities | R1 | INV-05, SCH-01 | SITE, STORE, PM
1. As site.a create a material request of 200 bags of cement for activity "Slab level 2" for next Monday.
2. As store.a reserve the quantity.
3. Try to issue the reserved bags to another project.
=> Available quantity (on hand minus reserved) is shown separately; the reservation is linked to the activity.
=> Issuing reserved stock to an unrelated demand is refused or requires releasing the reservation with a reason.

@case T-INV-06 | Batches, heat numbers and serial numbers with linked test certificates | R3 | INV-06, QA-04 | STORE, QA
1. Receive 20 tonnes of Y12 rebar and enter heat numbers H-4471 (12 t) and H-4472 (8 t); attach mill certificates.
2. Issue 5 tonnes from H-4471.
3. Search for where heat H-4471 went.
=> Stock is held per heat; issues name the heat; the certificate is attached to the batch.
=> The trace shows receipt, issues, and the cost code or work package that received it (forward traceability).

@case T-INV-07 | Reorder points, minimum/maximum levels and suggested requisitions | R1 | INV-07 | STORE, PROC
pre: Cement at ST-101: min 200 bags, max 1,500 bags, reorder point 300.
1. Issue stock until the system quantity is 180 bags.
2. Open the suggested requisitions.
=> A suggestion appears to buy up to the maximum: 1,500 - 180 = **1,320 bags**; the suggestion does not become a requisition until a person creates it.

@case T-INV-08 | Returns to supplier and surplus transfer between projects at valuation | R2 | INV-08 | STORE, PM, FIN
1. Return 20 damaged bags to S-01 against the original receipt.
2. Transfer 100 surplus bags from P-101 to P-102.
=> The return reduces stock at the original receipt cost, creates a debit note/claim against the supplier and cannot exceed the quantity received.
=> The surplus transfer charges P-102 and credits P-101 at the current valuation, with approval, and both project cost reports update.

@case T-INV-09 | Consumption is compared with the theoretical quantity from the BOQ; wastage reported | R2 | INV-09 | PM, STORE
pre: BOQ theory: 7 bags of cement per m3 of C-25 concrete. 100 m3 poured. 770 bags issued to 03-100.
1. Open the material consumption report.
=> Theoretical 700 bags; actual 770; **wastage 70 bags = 10%** with value at average cost; drill-down to the issues.

@case T-INV-10 | Barcode or QR labels and mobile scanning | R3 | INV-10, EI-HW-01 | STORE

2. Scan with the phone to open the item or confirm a receipt line.

@case T-INV-11 | Stock can never go negative and movements are immutable | R1 | INV-01, NFR-DAT-01, NFR-DAT-02 | STORE, UAT
1. With 1,100 bags in stock try to issue 1,200 bags.
2. Try to delete an issue document. Try to change its quantity.
3. Reverse the issue properly.
=> Step 1 is refused with the available quantity stated.
=> Step 2 is refused. Step 3 creates a reversal movement and journal; the original remains and both are visible.
