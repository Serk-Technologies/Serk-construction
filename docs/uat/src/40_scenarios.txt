# 29. End-to-end scenarios (SRS section 5 use cases UC-01 to UC-14)
@release R1

## How to run the scenarios
The earlier chapters test one control at a time. A scenario is a **story told across several roles**, the way a real month on project P-101 goes. Run scenarios in order UC-01 to UC-14 on a fresh copy of the fixture data: later scenarios reuse earlier documents (the budget created in UC-01, the stock received in UC-03, the certificate in UC-05). Every row has a person, an action and a figure to check. Where a figure is given as a formula, compute it from your starting numbers in the test data sheet (section 4.4). Tick the OK box when the row's result is exactly right; a scenario passes only if every row passes.

> Keep one running list of document numbers. In each scenario the "who" column tells you whose login to use. If a step needs a second person, the row says so.

# 30. Adversarial and attack scenarios (Master Specification v2.1 section 19, mapped to the SRS)

## Why this chapter exists
The best test of a control is to try to defeat it. Each case below is an attack or a nasty accident that a clever insider or a bad day could produce: forged approvals, replayed phones, cross-tenant probes, power cuts mid-transaction. The cases are grouped under the Master Specification's twelve scenarios A01-A12 and each is tied to the SRS requirements that make it testable. Most need the **vendor engineer** alongside a tester because they use fault injection or database access in a **test copy only**. Never run these on a production database.

@term Fault injection = Deliberately breaking part of the system (killing a process, cutting the network) to see whether the controls hold.
@term Replay = Re-sending an old valid request in the hope it is accepted again.

@case T-ADV-A01 | Two projects try to reserve the same crane at the same moment | R3 | EQP-06, SCH-04, EQP-02 | PM, EQP, UAT
pre: Master Specification v2.1 A01. The SRS asks for resource reservation through resource loading and equipment transfers; if shared-resource reservation is not delivered, mark Blocked.
1. As pm.a (P-101) and pm.b (P-102) both reserve crane TR-02 for the same Tuesday, pressing confirm at the same instant (vendor script).
=> One reservation is committed; the other is refused with an explanation naming the conflicting project and dates. No double booking exists in any report.

@case T-ADV-A02 | A maker/checker violation with a replayed or stale approval | R1 | IAM-08, WFL-03, SOD-T01 | UAT, FIN
1. Capture an approval request made by fin.b for journal J1 (vendor tool). Replay it for journal J2. Replay it after J1 has been changed.
2. Try the approval as the maker through a second device.
=> The replay for J2 and the stale replay are rejected: the attestation is bound to the exact document revision. The maker's attempt is refused. All attempts are audited.

@case T-ADV-A03 | Offline receiving replayed after a device has been revoked | R3 | SIT-02, SIT-04, IAM-11 | SITE, ADM, UAT
1. site.a records a goods receipt offline. Before reconnecting, adm.a revokes the device.
2. Reconnect and sync.
3. Replay the captured request from another machine.
=> The queued request is rejected; no stock or ledger movement occurs; both attempts are audited; the user is told the device is revoked. (Replay test SYN-T01.)

@case T-ADV-A04 | Supplier bank account substituted before a payment run | R1 | ORG-03, TRS-01, IAM-04 | PROC, TRS, CFO
1. As proc.a change S-04's bank account to a new one and submit. Then prepare a payment run including S-04's approved invoice.
2. Try to approve and release the payment before the new bank account is approved.
3. Have the second approver approve the account change with MFA; check the release.
=> Until the change is independently approved with MFA, payment to the new account is blocked (the payment either uses the old verified account or is held). The control review report shows the change and who approved it. The account number remains masked.

@case T-ADV-A05 | A posted journal is reversed after the period has closed | R1 | FIN-01, FIN-03 | FIN, CFO
1. Post journal J3 in P1; close P1.
2. Try to reverse J3 dated in P1; then dated in P2.
=> The original is never changed. A reversal dated in the closed period is refused; a reversal in the open period P2 is accepted according to period policy; the link between J3 and its reversal is visible. A reopened period requires the CFO and is audited.

@case T-ADV-A06 | Cross-tenant search, export, signed media link and AI request | R1 | IAM-01, RPT-02, DOC-08, AI-01, ISO-T01 | UAT
1. As a user of tenant Zambezi, try: global search for Abay terms; export with a crafted filter; reuse a signed media URL from Abay; ask the assistant about Abay data.
=> Zero disclosure on every route; each attempt is refused and logged with the tenant, user and route.

@case T-ADV-A07 | The site diary claims a quantity but a quality hold persists | R3 | QA-02, EVM-04, SUB-02 | SITE, QS, QA
pre: Hold point on steel inspection is unreleased (T-QA-02).
1. site.a records a diary entry stating 120 m3 of slab placed.
2. qs.a tries to include it in a measurement and in a subcontract certificate; pm.a tries to approve progress.
=> The diary is only evidence; no certified progress, no measurement acceptance and no payable arise while the hold stands; refusals name the hold point.

@case T-ADV-A08 | A budget revision and a PO award happen simultaneously | R1 | PCC-01, PCC-02, SOD-T02 | PM, PROC, UAT
pre: 03-200 remaining 800,000.
1. At the same moment (vendor script): one user approves a budget reduction of 600,000 on 03-200, another approves a PO of 500,000 on 03-200.
=> Exactly one succeeds; the other is refused with the budget numbers at that moment. Never does 03-200 end up with commitments above the approved budget without an override.

@case T-ADV-A09 | A database failure after the state change but before the outbox is delivered | R1 | AUD-02, INT-02, NFR-AVL-03 | UAT
1. Approve a PO and cut the database connection right after commit, before webhooks are sent; restart the services.
2. Check the PO, audit record, notifications and webhook.
=> The PO, audit entry and outbox event exist once (committed together); notifications and webhook are delivered after recovery, once. If the cut came before commit, none of them exist.

@case T-ADV-A10 | Region outage with multiple projects; restore with everything reconciled | R1 | NFR-AVL-02, NFR-AVL-05, OPS-T01 | UAT, ADM, FIN
1. Simulate loss of the whole primary region. Restore to the secondary location from encrypted off-site backups to a point in time.
2. Reconcile stock, AP, AR, cash and the audit chain for P-101 and P-102.
=> Meets the stated RPO/RTO; cash, stock, ledger and audit reconcile with zero difference; users can sign in again and no tenant sees another tenant's data.

@case T-ADV-A11 | A source drawing revision is replaced in the middle of work | R3 | DOC-02, SCH-06 | PM, SITE
1. A work package is "Ready" with drawing revision B. Publish revision C.
2. Open the work package.
=> The package is invalidated: marked "Not ready: drawing superseded", with the reason and the person who must re-confirm readiness.

@case T-ADV-A12 | A financial report and the assistant use stale projections | R1 | RPT-06, AI-01, AI-03 | FIN, EXE
1. Pause the projection refresh (vendor). Post a large journal. Run the cost report and ask the assistant for the cost of the project.
2. Resume the refresh.
=> The report and the answer both show data-freshness times and a staleness warning; the numbers are recomputed correctly once refreshed. Drill-down to the source recomputes from the ledger.
